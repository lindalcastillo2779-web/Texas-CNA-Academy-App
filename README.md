# Wedding Planning App

A premium bilingual wedding planning platform for couples, planners, collaborators, and guests.

## Vision
Wedding Planning App is being built as a luxury modern wedding operating system that combines elegant wedding experiences with powerful planning workflows. The platform is designed to support planning, guest communication, stationery, exports, collaboration, monetization, and intelligent automation in one connected product.

## Core product pillars
- Planning command center
- Budget and payments
- Guests, RSVP, and seating
- Vendors and timeline
- Wedding website and guest portal
- Stationery and digital asset systems
- Registry, thank-you tracking, and memories
- Collaboration and planner workflows
- Premium subscriptions and monetization
- AI assistance and automation

## Experience goals
- Premium and emotionally warm
- Beautiful but operationally strong
- Bilingual in English and Spanish
- Mobile-friendly, desktop-capable, and iPad-friendly
- Differentiated from generic planning tools and disconnected wedding templates
