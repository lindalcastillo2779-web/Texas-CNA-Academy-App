# Texas Health and Human Services (HHS / HHSC) – CNA Regulatory Overview

This folder contains Texas HHS/HHSC-related knowledge documents for Buddy.

## About Texas HHS

Texas Health and Human Services (HHS) is the umbrella agency responsible for health and
human services programs in Texas. The Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC) is the
administrative arm that oversees many of these programs.

HHSC regulates:
- Nurse aide training and certification programs (NATCEP)
- Long-term care facilities (nursing homes, assisted living)
- The Texas Nurse Aide Registry
- Background check requirements for healthcare workers

## NATCEP Program Approval

Training programs wishing to offer a state-approved CNA course must apply to HHSC for
NATCEP program approval. Requirements include:

- Qualified instructor (RN with minimum experience requirements)
- Approved curriculum meeting state hour requirements (minimum 75 total hours: 60 classroom/lab + 16 clinical, with some overlap allowed per state rules — **verify current hours with HHSC**)
- Clinical training site agreement with an approved long-term care facility
- Program policies and procedures meeting HHSC standards

## CNA Renewal Requirements

CNAs in Texas must renew certification every two years. To renew, a CNA must:
- Demonstrate active employment performing CNA duties during the renewal period, OR
- Complete continuing education (CE) hours as required (verify current CE requirements with HHSC)
- Maintain a clear record on the nurse aide registry

CNAs who have not worked in the past 24 months may need to complete a refresher course
or retest before renewing.

## Abuse, Neglect, and Exploitation (ANE)

HHSC investigates allegations of ANE against residents of long-term care facilities.
Substantiated findings are posted on the nurse aide registry and may result in:
- Revocation of CNA certification
- Disqualification from working in a regulated facility
