# Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) – CNA Training Resources

This folder contains TWC-related knowledge documents for Buddy.

## What is the TWC?

The Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) is a state agency that oversees workforce development,
unemployment insurance, and job training programs in Texas.

TWC is relevant to CNA training in several ways:
- **Skills Development Fund (SDF):** TWC administers grants that can fund CNA training
  for employed workers at healthcare facilities.
- **Workforce Solutions:** Local workforce boards partner with training providers to
  support CNA candidates through financial assistance and job placement.
- **Registered Apprenticeship:** TWC supports apprenticeship programs, including
  healthcare pathways that may include nurse aide training.

## Skills Development Fund (SDF)

The SDF provides grants to Texas employers and training providers to:
- Upgrade the skills of existing employees
- Train new hires for specific employers
- Support incumbent worker training

Healthcare facilities can apply for SDF grants to fund CNA training programs for their
current or prospective employees.

## Workforce Solutions Offices

Local Workforce Solutions offices can assist CNA candidates with:
- Career counseling and job placement
- Financial assistance for training fees (Income Support Division eligibility applies)
- Job search resources
