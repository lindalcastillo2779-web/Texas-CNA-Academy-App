# TULIP – Texas Unified Licensure Information Portal

This folder contains TULIP-related knowledge documents for Buddy.

## What is TULIP?

TULIP (Texas Unified Licensure Information Portal) is the online system managed by
Texas Health and Human Services (HHSC) for:

- CNA (Nurse Aide) Registry lookups
- Certified Nurse Aide listing and status verification
- Program reporting by approved NATCEP training providers
- Tracking of nurse aide training program completions

## Nurse Aide Registry

The Texas Nurse Aide Registry is maintained under HHSC. It lists:
- All CNAs who have completed an approved NATCEP program in Texas
- Their certification status (active, expired, revoked, etc.)
- Any findings related to abuse, neglect, or exploitation (ANE findings)

Facilities are required by law to check the nurse aide registry before hiring CNAs.

## Program Reporting

Approved NATCEP programs must report student completions and outcomes through TULIP.
Programs should report:
- Student enrollment
- Completion of classroom/lab training
- Completion of clinical hours
- Competency evaluation outcomes

## Verifying CNA Status

To verify a CNA's certification status:
1. Go to the Texas Nurse Aide Registry (via TULIP / HHSC website)
2. Search by name or Social Security Number
3. Review the certification status and any findings

Official resource: Texas Health and Human Services – Nurse Aide Registry
URL: https://www.hhs.texas.gov/providers/long-term-care-providers/nurse-aide-registry
