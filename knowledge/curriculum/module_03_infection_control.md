# Module 03: Infection Control Foundations

## Learning objectives

- Apply standard and transmission-based precautions consistently.
- Demonstrate correct hand hygiene and PPE sequencing.
- Report infection-control breaches and exposure events immediately.

## Regulatory references (Texas and federal)

- **42 CFR §483.80** - Infection prevention and control program requirements.
- **26 TAC §556.2** - Nurse aide training competencies for infection prevention.
- **CDC Standard Precautions Guidance** - Hand hygiene, PPE, and transmission controls.

## Chapter 1: Infection prevention principles

### Core instruction points
- Explain chain of infection and interruption strategies.
- Apply standard precautions in every resident contact.
- Differentiate clean and contaminated workflows.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Identifies all 6 chain-of-infection links and one break strategy each.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Chooses correct standard precaution actions in 3 care scenarios.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Segregates clean vs contaminated equipment correctly.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. Standard precautions apply to whom? **Answer: every resident, every encounter.**
2. One way to break transmission link is: **Answer: hand hygiene before and after care.**
3. Contaminated glove touches clean cart. Next action? **Answer: stop, disinfect, and replace contaminated items.**

**Case scenario**
- Learner identifies breach points in a wound-care setup and resets workflow.

**Remediation notes**
- Use chain-of-infection mapping worksheet.
- Repeat contamination tracing exercise.

## Chapter 2: Hand hygiene excellence

### Core instruction points
- Perform soap-and-water wash for at least 20 seconds when indicated.
- Use ABHR for routine decontamination when hands are not visibly soiled.
- Prioritize soap and water for C. diff exposure.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Demonstrates full-surface handwashing technique in sequence.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Selects ABHR vs soap-and-water correctly across 5 prompts.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Performs hand hygiene at all care transition points in simulation.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. C. diff contact requires: **Answer: soap and water.**
2. ABHR minimum alcohol concentration is: **Answer: 60%.**
3. Hand hygiene is required after glove removal? **Answer: yes, always.**

**Case scenario**
- Learner moves between two residents and must demonstrate timing-point hygiene.

**Remediation notes**
- Reassess with UV lotion coverage test.
- Practice WHO 5 moments adaptation for CNA workflow.

## Chapter 3: PPE selection and sequencing

### Core instruction points
- Match PPE to task risk and transmission pathway.
- Don and doff PPE in correct order.
- Avoid self-contamination during removal.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Selects correct PPE for droplet, contact, and splash scenarios.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Performs donning sequence without errors.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Performs doffing sequence without contaminating clothing/skin.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. First item to remove when doffing is: **Answer: gloves.**
2. Airborne precautions require: **Answer: N95 and room protocol compliance.**
3. Most common contamination moment is: **Answer: improper doffing.**

**Case scenario**
- Resident under contact precautions requires toileting assist; learner selects and removes PPE correctly.

**Remediation notes**
- Repeat don/doff drill at reduced speed with observer checklist.
- Correct hand placement errors before retest.

## Chapter 4: Transmission precautions and reporting

### Core instruction points
- Distinguish contact, droplet, and airborne signage.
- Use dedicated equipment and room workflow controls.
- Report breaches, exposures, and resident symptom changes promptly.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Identifies required precautions from door signage.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Executes room entry/exit workflow without cross-contamination.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Completes immediate exposure report with timeline and witness details.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. Influenza typically requires which precautions? **Answer: droplet.**
2. Shared equipment after isolation room should be: **Answer: disinfected before reuse.**
3. Exposure event reporting should occur: **Answer: immediately.**

**Case scenario**
- PPE tear during care; learner performs immediate decontamination and nurse notification.

**Remediation notes**
- Rehearse exposure protocol steps.
- Audit resident-room workflow for missed control points.
