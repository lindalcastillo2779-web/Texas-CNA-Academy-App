# Module 03: Infection Control Foundations

## Learning objectives

- Apply standard precautions across resident care scenarios.
- Choose and sequence PPE donning and doffing correctly.
- Use hand hygiene timing points to reduce transmission risk.

## Study checklist

1. Review standard vs. transmission-based precaution concepts.
2. Practice PPE order and disposal workflow.
3. Rehearse hand-hygiene moments before/after care activities.

## Skills focus

- Hand hygiene technique
- PPE selection for task risk
- Clean vs. contaminated surface awareness

## Ready for quiz when

- You can state where standard precautions always apply.
- You can list PPE removal order without prompts.
- You can explain why hand hygiene timing matters in CNA care.
