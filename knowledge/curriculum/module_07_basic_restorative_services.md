# Module 07: Basic Restorative Services

## Learning objectives

- Support resident independence with restorative care principles.
- Perform mobility and ROM assistance safely using proper body mechanics.
- Observe and report tolerance, pain, and functional changes during activity.

## Chapter 1: Restorative care principles and CNA role

- Explain how restorative care differs from total-care assistance.
- Reinforce care-plan goals for function and independence.
- Encourage resident participation rather than completing tasks for them.

## Chapter 2: Mobility support and safe ambulation

- Prepare environment and equipment before mobility tasks.
- Assist with gait belts, walkers, and wheelchairs per care plan.
- Monitor fatigue, dizziness, and instability during activity.

## Chapter 3: Range-of-motion and positioning routines

- Distinguish active vs. passive ROM and follow ordered techniques.
- Use proper alignment, joint protection, and slow controlled movement.
- Reposition to reduce pressure injury and contracture risk.

## Chapter 4: Documentation and restorative progress reporting

- Record distance, repetitions, level of assistance, and resident response.
- Report pain, refusal, weakness, or decline to the supervising nurse.
- Escalate safety concerns immediately when function changes abruptly.

## Study checklist

1. Practice gait-belt setup and transfer sequencing.
2. Review ROM safety limits and contraindication reminders.
3. Rehearse restorative charting language for functional changes.

## Skills focus

- Ambulation and transfer assistance
- ROM and positioning support
- Functional-progress observation and reporting

## Ready for quiz when

- You can identify safe restorative actions for common mobility scenarios.
- You can describe accurate restorative documentation elements.
- You can recognize warning signs requiring immediate nurse notification.
