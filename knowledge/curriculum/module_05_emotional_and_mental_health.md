# Module 05: Emotional and Mental Health Needs

## Learning objectives

- Identify psychosocial changes affecting resident outcomes.
- Use therapeutic communication to support emotional safety.
- Escalate mental health risk indicators immediately.

## Regulatory references (Texas and federal)

- **42 CFR §483.40** - Psychosocial and behavioral health services.
- **42 CFR §483.24** - Person-centered quality of life requirements.
- **42 CFR §483.12** - Protection from abuse and neglect including emotional harm.

## Chapter 1: Psychosocial wellness in long-term care

### Core instruction points
- Recognize social isolation, withdrawal, and adjustment stress.
- Support routines that preserve identity and autonomy.
- Track meaningful engagement indicators.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Identifies psychosocial decline cues in 3 resident examples.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Suggests two resident-centered engagement actions from care plan.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Reports mood/function change using objective language.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. Common psychosocial risk after admission is: **Answer: isolation and adjustment stress.**
2. CNA can support wellness by: **Answer: encouraging preferred routines and social contact.**
3. Best charting phrase includes: **Answer: observed behavior, not interpretation.**

**Case scenario**
- Previously social resident now refuses activities and meals.

**Remediation notes**
- Practice objective behavior charting.
- Revisit person-centered activity planning.

## Chapter 2: Therapeutic communication and listening

### Core instruction points
- Use open-ended questions and reflective statements.
- Allow silence and emotional processing.
- Maintain professional boundaries while showing empathy.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Uses two therapeutic prompts without giving false reassurance.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Demonstrates active listening with reflection and summary.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Avoids boundary-crossing responses in scenario practice.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. "Everything will be fine" is usually: **Answer: non-therapeutic reassurance.**
2. Best opening for upset resident: **Answer: "Tell me what you are feeling right now."**
3. Active listening includes: **Answer: reflection and clarification.**

**Case scenario**
- Resident expresses fear after new diagnosis; learner provides supportive response.

**Remediation notes**
- Replay conversation with therapeutic/non-therapeutic rubric.
- Coach neutral tone and pacing.

## Chapter 3: Grief, loss, and end-of-life support

### Core instruction points
- Respect varying grief responses across residents and families.
- Provide presence and comfort within CNA role.
- Escalate unresolved severe distress.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Identifies supportive CNA actions for grief scenario.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Uses culturally respectful language during end-of-life support role-play.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Escalates severe hopelessness signs immediately.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. Healthy grief response is always linear? **Answer: no.**
2. CNA role during grief includes: **Answer: presence, listening, and reporting concerns.**
3. Severe hopelessness should be: **Answer: reported promptly to nurse.**

**Case scenario**
- Resident after spouse death refuses interaction; learner balances support and escalation.

**Remediation notes**
- Review grief-stage myths and reality.
- Practice escalation phrasing for severe distress.

## Chapter 4: Mental health changes and urgent reporting

### Core instruction points
- Recognize suicidal ideation, hallucinations, and acute agitation.
- Follow immediate safety and reporting procedures.
- Document exact statements and observed behavior.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Correctly classifies urgent vs routine psychosocial changes.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Executes immediate reporting flow for suicidal statement.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Documents direct quotes and actions taken.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. Resident says "I want to die." CNA should: **Answer: stay with resident and notify nurse immediately.**
2. Hallucination report should include: **Answer: exact observed behavior and statements.**
3. Sudden severe confusion may indicate: **Answer: medical emergency.**

**Case scenario**
- Resident expresses self-harm thoughts during night care.

**Remediation notes**
- Drill immediate response sequence.
- Reassess documentation completeness with direct-quote requirement.
