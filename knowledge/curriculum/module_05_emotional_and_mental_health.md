# Module 05: Emotional and Mental Health Needs

## Learning objectives

- Identify psychosocial needs that affect resident well-being and outcomes.
- Use therapeutic communication to support anxiety, grief, and loneliness.
- Report mental-status changes, safety concerns, and self-harm cues quickly.

## Chapter 1: Psychosocial wellness in long-term care

- Understand adjustment stress after admission and major health changes.
- Recognize signs of social isolation and emotional decline.
- Promote routines that preserve identity, purpose, and connection.

## Chapter 2: Therapeutic communication and active listening

- Use open-ended prompts, reflection, and supportive silence.
- Respond to sadness, fear, and anger without judgment.
- Maintain professional boundaries while building trust.

## Chapter 3: Grief, loss, and end-of-life support

- Provide presence and comfort during anticipatory grief and bereavement.
- Respect resident/family coping styles and cultural expressions of grief.
- Escalate unresolved distress, hopelessness, or severe withdrawal to nursing staff.

## Chapter 4: Mental health changes and urgent reporting

- Recognize warning signs: sudden confusion, suicidal statements, hallucinations.
- Follow immediate reporting pathways for psychiatric safety concerns.
- Document exact resident statements and observed behaviors clearly.

## Study checklist

1. Practice therapeutic responses for anxiety and grief scenarios.
2. Review safety-trigger statements requiring immediate escalation.
3. Rehearse objective documentation for behavior and mood changes.

## Skills focus

- Emotional support communication
- Observation of mood/behavior shifts
- Timely nurse notification for mental health risk

## Ready for quiz when

- You can distinguish therapeutic from non-therapeutic communication.
- You can identify urgent psychosocial red flags.
- You can chart objective psychosocial observations without interpretation.
