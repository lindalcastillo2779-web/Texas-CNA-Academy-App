# Module 04: Care of Cognitively Impaired Residents

## Learning objectives

- Differentiate common cognitive conditions and urgent changes.
- Use person-centered communication and de-escalation techniques.
- Prevent wandering and harm through structured supervision.

## Regulatory references (Texas and federal)

- **42 CFR §483.40** - Behavioral health and psychosocial services.
- **42 CFR §483.25** - Quality of care and supervision standards.
- **Texas Health and Safety Code Chapter 247/250 guidance** - Resident protection and supervision in facility care.

## Chapter 1: Dementia and cognitive change fundamentals

### Core instruction points
- Distinguish dementia, delirium, and depression cues.
- Compare baseline cognition to acute changes.
- Prioritize immediate reporting for sudden confusion.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Classifies 3 cognitive-change scenarios correctly.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Identifies acute delirium red flags requiring urgent nurse report.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Documents baseline-vs-change findings objectively.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. Sudden confusion is most consistent with: **Answer: delirium (urgent).**
2. Dementia progression is usually: **Answer: gradual.**
3. First response to acute confusion: **Answer: ensure safety and notify nurse immediately.**

**Case scenario**
- Resident who is usually calm becomes acutely disoriented and pulling lines.

**Remediation notes**
- Revisit baseline comparison charting method.
- Repeat urgency triage drill.

## Chapter 2: Communication for confusion and memory loss

### Core instruction points
- Use short one-step directions and calm tone.
- Redirect without arguing or shaming.
- Use validation when emotions are intense.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Demonstrates one-step cueing in role-play.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Uses validation statement before redirection.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Avoids confrontation language in all scripted interactions.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. Best response to repeated question is: **Answer: calm repetition with reassurance.**
2. Arguing with delusion-like statement is: **Answer: non-therapeutic.**
3. Communication goal is to: **Answer: reduce distress and maintain safety.**

**Case scenario**
- Resident insists they must leave for work; learner responds with validation + redirect.

**Remediation notes**
- Practice script bank for common repetitive statements.
- Coach tone and pace under stress.

## Chapter 3: Behavior support and de-escalation

### Core instruction points
- Identify unmet needs behind behavior.
- Use non-pharmacologic interventions first.
- Escalate persistent unsafe behavior rapidly.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Identifies likely unmet need in 4 behavior vignettes.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Applies two de-escalation techniques before escalation.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Initiates urgent report for violence/self-harm indicators.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. Agitation before meals often signals: **Answer: unmet need (pain, hunger, toileting).**
2. First-line CNA behavior strategy is: **Answer: calm redirection and environment adjustment.**
3. Threatening behavior requires: **Answer: immediate safety response and nurse notification.**

**Case scenario**
- Resident becomes combative during evening care; learner re-approaches with safer method.

**Remediation notes**
- Replay scenario with trigger-identification checklist.
- Reinforce stop-and-escalate threshold.

## Chapter 4: Wandering prevention and safety supervision

### Core instruction points
- Maintain environmental safeguards for wandering risk.
- Follow elopement and missing-resident protocol.
- Document triggers and interventions clearly.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Completes wandering-risk room scan and secures exits per policy.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** States missing-resident escalation sequence accurately.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Logs trigger-intervention-response in objective format.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. Highest immediate risk behavior is: **Answer: unsupervised exit-seeking.**
2. If resident is missing, CNA should: **Answer: activate facility protocol immediately.**
3. Documentation should include: **Answer: trigger, intervention, resident response, who notified.**

**Case scenario**
- Shift handoff reveals repeated exit-seeking at dusk; learner builds prevention plan.

**Remediation notes**
- Drill missing-resident response timing.
- Reassess environment setup with pre-shift checklist.
