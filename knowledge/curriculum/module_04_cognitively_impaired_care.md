# Module 04: Care of Cognitively Impaired Residents

## Learning objectives

- Describe common causes and signs of cognitive impairment in long-term care.
- Apply redirection, validation, and cueing techniques during resident interactions.
- Prevent wandering, elopement, and agitation-related safety events.

## Chapter 1: Dementia and cognitive change fundamentals

- Distinguish dementia, delirium, and depression warning patterns.
- Recognize baseline-vs-change observations to report promptly.
- Protect dignity while assisting with memory-related deficits.

## Chapter 2: Communication strategies for confusion and memory loss

- Use short, calm, one-step instructions and visual cues.
- Avoid arguing; reorient gently when appropriate.
- Use validation therapy language during emotional distress.

## Chapter 3: Behavior support and de-escalation

- Identify unmet needs behind behaviors (pain, hunger, toileting, fear).
- Use non-pharmacologic calming routines and environmental adjustments.
- Escalate persistent or unsafe behavior changes to the nurse immediately.

## Chapter 4: Safety supervision and wandering prevention

- Perform routine environment scans for wandering and fall risks.
- Follow unit elopement and missing-resident response procedures.
- Document triggers, interventions, and resident responses objectively.

## Study checklist

1. Practice de-escalation phrasing for agitation scenarios.
2. Review when behavior changes require urgent nurse notification.
3. Rehearse wandering-prevention and supervision protocols.

## Skills focus

- Person-centered dementia communication
- Behavior observation and reporting
- Safety checks for cognitively impaired residents

## Ready for quiz when

- You can select an appropriate response to common dementia behaviors.
- You can explain how to report behavior changes using objective language.
- You can identify immediate safety steps for wandering risk.
