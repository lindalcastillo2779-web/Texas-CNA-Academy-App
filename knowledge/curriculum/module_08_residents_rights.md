# Module 08: Residents' Rights

## Learning objectives

- Apply OBRA and Texas resident-rights protections in daily care.
- Preserve dignity, privacy, and informed choice during all ADLs.
- Identify and report abuse, neglect, exploitation, and rights violations.

## Regulatory references (Texas and federal)

- **42 CFR §483.10** - Resident rights and informed participation.
- **42 CFR §483.12** - Freedom from abuse, neglect, and exploitation.
- **Texas Health and Safety Code Chapter 242 and HHSC LTC rules** - Resident protections and grievance expectations.

## Chapter 1: Legal foundations of resident rights

### Core instruction points
- Review autonomy, privacy, and participation rights.
- Align daily care actions with resident choices and care plan.
- Protect resident property and communication rights.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Correctly identifies rights violated in 4 scenarios.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** States resident right to refuse care and required CNA follow-up.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Explains confidentiality protections in common workflow settings.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. Primary federal rights framework for LTC residents is in: **Answer: 42 CFR §483.10.**
2. Residents may refuse treatment? **Answer: yes, with proper notification/documentation workflow.**
3. Property loss concerns should be: **Answer: reported and documented promptly.**

**Case scenario**
- Resident refuses bath and requests different schedule; learner protects rights and reports.

**Remediation notes**
- Rehearse rights-based response scripts.
- Review consent/refusal documentation fields.

## Chapter 2: Dignity, privacy, and consent in care

### Core instruction points
- Knock, introduce, explain, and obtain consent before care.
- Use draping and door/curtain privacy safeguards.
- Offer choices whenever clinically safe.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Performs privacy-and-consent sequence before personal care.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Maintains draping/privacy throughout simulated ADL care.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Offers at least two resident-choice options appropriately.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. Best first action before personal care: **Answer: explain and obtain consent.**
2. Privacy includes: **Answer: physical, verbal, and informational privacy.**
3. If resident declines care now, CNA should: **Answer: respect, reschedule if possible, notify nurse.**

**Case scenario**
- Shared room care interaction requires privacy and consent with limited space.

**Remediation notes**
- Repeat mock ADL setup with privacy rubric.
- Coach choice-offering language.

## Chapter 3: Abuse prevention and mandatory reporting

### Core instruction points
- Recognize physical, emotional, sexual, financial abuse and neglect.
- Ensure immediate resident safety.
- Report suspected abuse through required channels without delay.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Identifies abuse subtype from observation examples.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Initiates immediate safety-and-reporting sequence in simulation.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Documents objective evidence and witness information correctly.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. CNA who suspects abuse should: **Answer: report immediately per policy and law.**
2. Waiting for proof before reporting is: **Answer: incorrect and unsafe.**
3. Emotional abuse can include: **Answer: threats, humiliation, or intimidation.**

**Case scenario**
- Learner witnesses rough handling during transfer and must escalate promptly.

**Remediation notes**
- Drill mandatory-reporting decision tree.
- Review objective abuse documentation examples.

## Chapter 4: Grievance process and advocacy

### Core instruction points
- Encourage residents to voice concerns safely.
- Route grievances through facility process.
- Collaborate with social services and nursing for resolution.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Explains grievance pathway to resident in plain language.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Submits complete complaint handoff to nurse/supervisor.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Demonstrates non-retaliatory support behavior in role-play.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. Residents can file grievances without retaliation? **Answer: yes.**
2. CNA role in grievance includes: **Answer: listening, documenting, and routing concerns appropriately.**
3. If grievance is unresolved, CNA should: **Answer: escalate through policy channels.**

**Case scenario**
- Resident reports repeated call-light delays and fears retaliation.

**Remediation notes**
- Practice grievance intake script.
- Reinforce escalation timeline and follow-up documentation.
