# Module 08: Residents' Rights

## Learning objectives

- Explain core resident rights protected under OBRA and Texas facility standards.
- Apply dignity, privacy, and informed-choice practices in daily care tasks.
- Recognize and report abuse, neglect, exploitation, and rights violations.

## Chapter 1: Legal foundations of resident rights

- Review rights to autonomy, informed participation, and freedom from abuse.
- Understand CNA obligations for confidentiality and respectful conduct.
- Follow policy for protecting resident property and personal choices.

## Chapter 2: Dignity, privacy, and consent in care delivery

- Knock, introduce yourself, and explain care before beginning tasks.
- Use draping and privacy protections during personal care.
- Support resident decisions unless immediate safety risk requires escalation.

## Chapter 3: Abuse prevention and mandatory reporting

- Identify physical, verbal, sexual, financial, and neglect indicators.
- Follow immediate reporting chain for any suspected abuse event.
- Preserve resident safety and document objective observations promptly.

## Chapter 4: Grievance process and advocacy

- Encourage residents to voice concerns without fear of retaliation.
- Route complaints through facility grievance channels per policy.
- Partner with nurses and social services to support resident advocacy.

## Study checklist

1. Review abuse-warning signs and mandatory reporting timelines.
2. Practice consent and privacy language before personal-care tasks.
3. Rehearse objective charting for rights-related incidents.

## Skills focus

- Rights-preserving communication
- Abuse/neglect recognition and escalation
- Resident advocacy and grievance support

## Ready for quiz when

- You can identify resident-rights violations in scenario questions.
- You can describe immediate reporting steps for suspected abuse.
- You can explain how dignity and informed choice shape routine CNA care.
