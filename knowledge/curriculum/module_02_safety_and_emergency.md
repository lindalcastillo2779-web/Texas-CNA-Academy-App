# Module 02: Safety and Emergency Response

## Learning objectives

- Apply fall-prevention and environmental safety fundamentals.
- Execute immediate CNA-level emergency responses within scope.
- Report and document incidents accurately and without delay.

## Regulatory references (Texas and federal)

- **26 TAC §556.2** - Safety competencies in nurse aide instruction.
- **42 CFR §483.25(d)** - Accident hazards and supervision requirements.
- **42 CFR §483.12** - Freedom from abuse, neglect, and exploitation.

## Chapter 1: Environmental hazard prevention

### Core instruction points
- Perform room-entry hazard scan every encounter.
- Correct high-risk conditions: wet floors, poor lighting, tubing clutter.
- Verify call light access, bed height, and non-skid footwear.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Completes a 60-second room hazard scan with zero missed critical risks.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Corrects at least 3 hazards before initiating care.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** States immediate escalation criteria for unresolved hazards.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. Most preventable fall trigger in resident rooms? **Answer: clutter and poor setup.**
2. First action when floor is wet near resident bed? **Answer: secure area and remove hazard immediately.**
3. Call light should be: **Answer: within resident reach at all times.**

**Case scenario**
- Learner enters cluttered room before transfer and must reprioritize safety setup.

**Remediation notes**
- Repeat hazard-recognition drills with timed observation cards.
- Use photo-based hazard identification until 90% accuracy.

## Chapter 2: Safe movement and transfer readiness

### Core instruction points
- Verify mobility status and assist level in care plan.
- Prepare equipment and lock wheels before movement.
- Stop transfer and call for help if resident becomes unstable.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Performs pre-transfer safety check (wheel locks, gait belt, bed height).
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Demonstrates safe body mechanics throughout transfer simulation.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Aborts transfer appropriately when safety criteria fail.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. If wheelchair brakes are unlocked before transfer, CNA should: **Answer: stop and lock before proceeding.**
2. Correct transfer priority is: **Answer: resident safety over speed.**
3. Sign of transfer intolerance includes: **Answer: dizziness and knee buckling.**

**Case scenario**
- Resident reports dizziness mid-transfer; learner applies emergency stabilization and escalation.

**Remediation notes**
- Repractice gait-belt placement and pivot sequencing.
- Reassess stop-rule decision-making with instructor cues.

## Chapter 3: Emergency response framework

### Core instruction points
- Apply RACE and PASS for fire events.
- Recognize choking, collapse, and acute respiratory distress triggers.
- Activate facility emergency pathway immediately.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Recites RACE and PASS steps in correct order.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Initiates immediate nurse alert in simulated collapse.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Maintains resident safety while awaiting licensed response.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. First step in RACE is: **Answer: rescue from immediate danger.**
2. Severe choking in conscious adult requires: **Answer: immediate abdominal thrust protocol per policy.**
3. During emergency, CNA documents after: **Answer: resident is stabilized and report made.**

**Case scenario**
- Dining-room choking event; learner initiates response and communication sequence.

**Remediation notes**
- Repeat emergency algorithms with flash-card prompts.
- Validate verbal call-for-help script under time pressure.

## Chapter 4: Post-incident reporting

### Core instruction points
- Stay with resident and monitor status after event.
- Report objective timeline to supervising nurse.
- Document findings, interventions, and resident response.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Produces complete incident timeline with no missing critical fields.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Uses objective language without blame statements.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Identifies required notifications per facility policy.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. Best charting detail after fall: **Answer: location, observed condition, action taken, nurse notified.**
2. Incident reports should include assumptions? **Answer: no, objective facts only.**
3. Resident should be left alone after incident? **Answer: no, remain with resident unless directed otherwise.**

**Case scenario**
- Learner completes post-fall report and handoff to incoming shift.

**Remediation notes**
- Compare strong vs weak incident reports.
- Repeat charting with objective-only language rubric.
