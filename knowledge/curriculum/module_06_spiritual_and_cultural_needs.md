# Module 06: Spiritual and Cultural Needs

## Learning objectives

- Provide culturally humble, preference-based CNA care.
- Support spiritual practices safely within care-plan boundaries.
- Document and report accommodation needs for team continuity.

## Regulatory references (Texas and federal)

- **42 CFR §483.10** - Resident dignity, self-determination, and participation rights.
- **42 CFR §483.24** - Person-centered care planning requirements.
- **Title VI Civil Rights Act guidance in healthcare settings** - Language access and nondiscrimination expectations.

## Chapter 1: Cultural humility in CNA practice

### Core instruction points
- Ask, do not assume, resident preferences.
- Respect language, food, privacy, and touch preferences.
- Align care routines with individual identity and dignity.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Uses preference-based intake prompts in role-play.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Identifies and corrects stereotype-based care errors.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Documents one cultural preference affecting ADL care.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. Cultural humility means: **Answer: ongoing learning and resident-led preference inquiry.**
2. Best first step when unsure of preference: **Answer: ask resident respectfully.**
3. Assumptions in care planning increase: **Answer: risk of mistrust and poor outcomes.**

**Case scenario**
- Resident declines standard meal option for cultural reasons.

**Remediation notes**
- Practice neutral, respectful preference questions.
- Review examples of bias-aware language.

## Chapter 2: Spiritual support and accommodation

### Core instruction points
- Recognize spiritual distress and requests for ritual support.
- Facilitate clergy/prayer items per policy.
- Escalate conflicts between safety orders and spiritual practices.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Identifies spiritually supportive CNA actions within scope.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Routes accommodation request through correct interdisciplinary channel.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Maintains dignity when accommodation cannot be granted immediately.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. CNA may support spirituality by: **Answer: facilitating approved rituals and referrals.**
2. If request conflicts with treatment, CNA should: **Answer: notify nurse and team for care-plan decision.**
3. Resident spiritual choices are part of: **Answer: person-centered rights.**

**Case scenario**
- Resident requests prayer time that overlaps with scheduled care.

**Remediation notes**
- Rehearse escalation script for conflict scenarios.
- Review scope boundaries for spiritual facilitation.

## Chapter 3: Cross-cultural communication and interpretation

### Core instruction points
- Use plain language and teach-back.
- Use qualified interpreters per policy.
- Avoid relying on assumptions or untrained translation.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Performs teach-back with clear, short instructions.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Activates interpreter process correctly.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Documents communication supports used during care.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. Preferred approach for language barrier is: **Answer: qualified interpreter.**
2. Teach-back confirms: **Answer: resident understanding.**
3. Family member as translator is always preferred? **Answer: no.**

**Case scenario**
- New resident has limited English proficiency during transfer teaching.

**Remediation notes**
- Repeat teach-back practice with translated prompts.
- Audit interpreter activation steps.

## Chapter 4: Inclusive care planning and conflict resolution

### Core instruction points
- Communicate resident preferences to interdisciplinary team.
- Resolve care conflicts with respect and safety focus.
- Track outcomes of accommodation requests.

### Skill checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Summarizes preference concern to nurse/social worker clearly.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Uses de-escalation language during family-team disagreement.
- [ ] **PASS/FAIL:** Documents agreed care adaptation and follow-up plan.

### Assessment content
**Quiz bank**
1. Best outcome for preference conflict is: **Answer: documented team-supported plan.**
2. CNA advocacy should center on: **Answer: resident rights and safety.**
3. Unresolved conflict should be: **Answer: escalated, not ignored.**

**Case scenario**
- Family and resident disagree on grooming preference; learner coordinates respectful team response.

**Remediation notes**
- Role-play conflict conversations with boundary cues.
- Rebuild documentation using preference-impact-action format.
