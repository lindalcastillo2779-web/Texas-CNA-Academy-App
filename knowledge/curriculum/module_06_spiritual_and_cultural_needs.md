# Module 06: Spiritual and Cultural Needs

## Learning objectives

- Provide care that respects each resident's beliefs, values, and traditions.
- Adapt routines to support spiritual practices and cultural preferences safely.
- Communicate respectfully with interpreters, families, and diverse care teams.

## Chapter 1: Cultural humility in CNA practice

- Distinguish cultural humility from assumptions and stereotypes.
- Ask preference-based questions for personal care and routines.
- Honor food, dress, language, and privacy preferences when possible.

## Chapter 2: Spiritual support and religious accommodation

- Recognize spiritual distress and opportunities for supportive presence.
- Facilitate clergy visits, prayer items, or rituals per facility policy.
- Report conflicts between care plans and resident spiritual preferences.

## Chapter 3: Cross-cultural communication and interpretation

- Use simple language and teach-back for clarity and safety.
- Follow interpreter procedures instead of relying on assumptions.
- Document resident preferences and communication needs consistently.

## Chapter 4: Inclusive care planning and conflict resolution

- Collaborate with nurses when preferences affect treatment routines.
- Handle misunderstandings respectfully and escalate unresolved concerns.
- Protect resident rights while balancing safety and policy requirements.

## Study checklist

1. Review examples of culturally sensitive personal-care adaptations.
2. Practice language for discussing spiritual and cultural preferences.
3. Rehearse when to escalate accommodation barriers to nursing staff.

## Skills focus

- Preference-centered care communication
- Spiritual accommodation support
- Collaborative reporting for culturally complex care scenarios

## Ready for quiz when

- You can identify culturally responsive CNA actions in common scenarios.
- You can describe safe spiritual accommodations within CNA scope.
- You can document preferences clearly for team continuity.
