# Texas CNA Curriculum Overview

This folder contains curriculum-related knowledge documents for Buddy.

## Texas NATCEP Domains

The Texas Nurse Aide Training and Competency Evaluation Program (NATCEP) covers the
following domains:

1. **Role of the Nurse Aide** – responsibilities, scope of practice, ethics, legal rights
2. **Promotion of Safety** – accident/injury prevention, fire safety, infection control
3. **Promotion of Function and Health of Residents** – ADLs, mobility, personal care
4. **Care of Cognitively Impaired Residents** – dementia, communication, behavior management
5. **Emotional and Mental Health Needs** – psychosocial care, grieving, mental health
6. **Spiritual and Cultural Needs** – cultural competency, religious preferences
7. **Basic Restorative Services** – rehabilitation, assistive devices, range of motion
8. **Residents' Rights** – OBRA rights, dignity, privacy, grievance process

## Clinical Skills

Texas NATCEP requires a minimum of 60 hours of classroom/lab training and 16 hours of
supervised clinical experience.

Key clinical skills include:
- Hand hygiene (handwashing and hand sanitizer use)
- Donning and doffing PPE (gloves, gown, mask, eye protection)
- Bedmaking (occupied and unoccupied beds)
- Oral care (conscious and unconscious resident)
- Perineal care
- Catheter care
- Range-of-motion exercises (active and passive)
- Positioning (supine, lateral, Fowler's, semi-Fowler's)
- Transfers (bed to chair, using gait belt)
- Vital signs (temperature, pulse, respiration, blood pressure)
- Measuring intake and output
- Feeding and assisting with meals
- Applying restraints (following current Texas/federal guidance)
- Reporting and documentation

## Exam Structure

The Texas NATCEP competency evaluation consists of:
- **Written/oral knowledge test** – multiple-choice questions
- **Skills demonstration** – candidate performs randomly selected clinical skills before an evaluator

Candidates must pass both components. The written test is administered by Prometric on behalf of HHSC.
