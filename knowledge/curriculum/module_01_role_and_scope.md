# Module 01: Role of the Nurse Aide

## Learning objectives

- Define CNA scope of practice in Texas NATCEP settings.
- Explain delegation boundaries and when to report to a nurse.
- Demonstrate resident-rights aware communication and documentation habits.

## Study checklist

1. Review scope limits vs. nurse-only tasks.
2. Practice proper reporting flow: observe, document, escalate.
3. Memorize key resident rights: dignity, privacy, informed choice.

## Skills focus

- Professional communication with residents and care teams
- Timely observation reporting
- Accurate, objective charting language

## Ready for quiz when

- You can identify whether a task is in CNA scope.
- You can describe who to notify for urgent resident changes.
- You can explain how resident rights guide daily care.
